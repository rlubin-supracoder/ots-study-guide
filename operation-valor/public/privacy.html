✦OPERATION VALOR
LOCATION & PRIVACY

Your check-in
is deliberate.

Operation Valor is a location check-in tool for the OTS land navigation exercise. It does not continuously track you.

What is shared

When you press Send my location, the page asks your device for a position. A submitted check-in includes your code name, exercise number, latitude, longitude, reported accuracy, capture time, and server receipt time. Your latest check-in and total check-in count are stored on Cloudflare and visible to authorized exercise controllers. Participants cannot view the exercise roster or other participants’ locations.

Your choice

You can deny location permission or close the page. No location is requested merely by joining. If the five-meter accuracy target is not reached, you may retry or explicitly choose to send an approximate position. The browser’s accuracy value is an estimate, not a guarantee.

Storage and clearing

The app keeps only your latest position, not a route history. The exercise controller can end the exercise and clear its roster and positions. The app automatically clears this data 24 hours after the exercise starts. Cloudflare’s infrastructure backups may retain recoverable storage for a limited period after app-level clearing.

A private, first-party cookie identifies your participant session in this browser for up to 24 hours. Your location is not stored in that cookie or in browser local storage. No advertising or analytics scripts are used.

Controller map

The controller dashboard loads map tiles from OpenStreetMap. That service receives standard web requests, including the displayed map area and controller IP address, but the app does not send it code names or participant records. Its map tiles follow the OpenStreetMap tile policy.
